Privacy

Español · Shadow · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Shadow is a game: a little figure walks on its own and you move the sun, so that its shadow becomes a bridge. It is built to know nothing about you: it has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: levels completed, records, challenges, stars and constellations.
  • Your garden, your sparks (✦) and the outfits you have unlocked with them.
  • Your journey in free mode.
  • Your settings (music, sound effects, haptics, difficulty and language).

All of this is saved only on your device. The developer has no access to it. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect any data: nothing leaves your device.
  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics or usage logs.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

The game does not ask for or store personal data, so it is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If the game changes what it does with your data, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
