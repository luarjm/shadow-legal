Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
The figure walks on its own. Drag your finger anywhere on the screen to move the sun (or the moon at night). The shadow's tip has to land on solid ground on the other side of each gap.

The figure stopped. Why?
When the sun is in front of it, it waits. Move the sun behind it and it walks on.

It fell. Have I lost?
No. It goes back to the last beacon (or the start of the level) and you try again.

Can I make it easier?
Yes: Settings › Difficulty › Easy. On Easy, a guide shows where the shadow's tip will land.

There is no sound.
Check the silent switch and the volume, and that music and sound effects are on in Settings.

Is there anything to buy?
No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. Sparks (✦) are earned by playing.
